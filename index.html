How it is made

Slow, on purpose.

The old way. Copper, a low fire and a lot of waiting. Three weeks from flower to bottle.

  2. 01

    Picked before sunrise

    Flowers hold the most oil in the early morning. They are picked before the heat and reach the copper the same day.

  3. 02

    Distilled over a low fire

    Steam carries the scent into a base of sandalwood oil. No alcohol and nothing added to push it louder.

  4. 03

    Rested for two weeks

    The oil settles and rounds out. Then every bottle is filled, sealed and checked by hand.
